# Code of Conduct

## Our community

Astro Solitude welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate. English and Chinese contributions are welcome.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and explain your reasoning.
- Respect different perspectives, accept corrections, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy and ask before sharing their personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces, and when someone represents Astro Solitude in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [everfu](https://github.com/everfu), at [o@efu.me](mailto:o@efu.me). Include relevant links, a description of what happened, and any context that can help assess the report. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as possible. Information may be shared with people needed to investigate or address the incident; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a correction, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. They should explain moderation decisions to the people affected when practical and safe.

To request reconsideration, contact the same address privately with relevant context. Reports and appeals should be considered fairly and without retaliation.
